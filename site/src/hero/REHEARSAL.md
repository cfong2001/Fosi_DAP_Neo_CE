# Rehearsal: DAP Neo header

Starting point: existing Stageshow upload hero. Kept its Stage fitting, theme,
visibility playback, compact layout and reduced-motion poster. Borrowed the
system-event structure from the skill's subscriptions example; replaced the
file window with a CSS 3D physical player modeled from the supplied photographs.
The requested device takes precedence over the skill's generic phone geometry.

Beats: idle, incoming RKNANOFWIMG, impact and full turn, illuminated CE screen.
Plays once in view and holds the result; no automatic rewind.

Validation: TypeScript and production build pass. Chromium captures at 1280 and
390 reviewed for opening, incoming file, spin and payoff. Pointer tracking,
page overflow, browser errors and dark reduced-motion poster checked separately.
The compiled docs/stage assets are rebuilt.

Film gate: did not pass. Sampling gaps made motion coverage inconclusive.
The gate treats the modeled casing as UI cards: flags unequal physical face
insets, protruding side buttons, and small engraved rear lettering. These are
intentional device geometry, not form controls. Phone top clearance and flight
endpoint were adjusted after the first capture. Boot text retains a compositing
layer. Spin geometry creates jump/handoff candidates in the DOM scan, although
all physical layers remain mounted and share the same rotation.

Not checked: physical mobile hardware, Safari and Firefox, GPU-specific rendering.
